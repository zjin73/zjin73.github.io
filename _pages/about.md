<section class="section about"><div><div class="eyebrow">About me</div><h2>Control research,<br>grounded in engineering.</h2></div><div><p>I am pursuing a master's degree at <strong>Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics</strong>, supervised by <strong>Dan Yu</strong>. I received my BEng in Aircraft Design and Engineering from Shenyang Aerospace University in 2023, after beginning my undergraduate studies in Mechanical Engineering.</p><p>My research includes <strong>reactive flow-diffusion for manipulator motion planning</strong> and <strong>Koopman bilinear model predictive control</strong>. My engineering experience spans robotic visual servoing, UAV inspection, and experimental temperature control for ceramic heating plates.</p></div></section>
