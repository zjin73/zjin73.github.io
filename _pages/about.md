Zhiheng Jin is a Master's student in **Control Science and Engineering** at **Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics**, expecting to graduate in **April 2027**. His research focuses on learning-based robotic motion planning, multi-arm coordination, generative trajectory planning, model predictive control, and data-driven dynamical modeling. His broader interests include uncertainty-aware decision-making, multi-agent systems, and intelligent control of complex dynamical systems.

He received his bachelor's degree in **Aircraft Design and Engineering** from **Shenyang Aerospace University**.

## Research Experience

- **Multi-arm motion planning:** Research involving coordinated motion planning and generative trajectory methods using Diffusion and Flow Matching.
- **Model predictive control:** Research involving trajectory tracking and constrained control of robotic systems.
- **Data-driven modeling and system identification:** Research involving Koopman-based dynamical modeling and its use in predictive control.

## Current Research Interests

- Autonomous decision-making under uncertainty, including partially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs)
- Multi-agent cooperation and coordinated decision-making
- Intelligent control of robots and complex dynamical systems

## PhD Opportunities

He is seeking PhD opportunities for **2027 entry**, with an interest in connecting his background in planning and control with decision-making under uncertainty and multi-agent cooperation.
