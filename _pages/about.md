<section class="section about-grid" aria-labelledby="about-title"><div><div class="eyebrow">01 / ABOUT</div><h2 id="about-title">Control as a foundation.<br>Autonomy as a goal.</h2></div><div><p>I am a master's student in <strong>Control Science and Engineering at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA)</strong>. My research experience spans model predictive control, data-driven system modelling, and motion planning for robotic manipulators.</p><p>I am interested in connecting <strong>physical control with sequential decision-making</strong>: how models can be learned and updated, how agents can coordinate with limited information, and how planned actions can remain feasible under uncertainty and shared constraints.</p><p>My long-term goal is to turn research in control and AI into practical technologies that improve industrial automation.</p></div></section>
